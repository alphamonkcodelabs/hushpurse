# HushPurse Privacy Policy

_Effective October 25, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the HushPurse app (the “App”), published by amsimplythebest (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: the App stores everything on your device, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal or financial information.

## Information we collect

None. The App has no account or sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. The current Android release does not even request permission to use the internet.

## Information stored on your device

The transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, recurring entries, people and notes you enter, and your settings, are saved in a database on your device. They are used only to show you your own records. We cannot access them.

## Your device’s backup

If your device is set to back up apps to your Google account (Android) or iCloud (iOS), your device may include the App’s data in that backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is made by your device’s operating system, stored in your own account, and controlled by you and Google or Apple. We cannot access it. You can turn this off in your device settings.

## Exports and backups you create

When you export a CSV file or create a backup, the App creates the file on your device and opens your device’s share menu. You choose where it goes, for example your email, a cloud drive or a messaging app. We do not receive these files. Once shared, they are handled under the privacy policy of the service you chose.

## Device permissions

The App uses:

- Biometrics / screen lock: only if you turn on App lock. Your device checks your fingerprint, face or PIN and tells the App only whether it succeeded. The App never sees or stores biometric data.
- Vibration: for small haptic feedback when you tap.
- Files: only when you choose a backup file to restore, through your device’s file picker.

## Advertising and purchases in the future

The App currently has no ads and no purchases. We may add them later. If we add advertising, ad providers may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, approximate location and how you interact with ads. Before that happens, we will update this policy to name those providers and describe what they collect, update the store’s data safety information, and ask for your consent in the App where the law requires it.

If we add paid features, payments will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation of your purchase, never your payment card or bank details.

Your financial records will stay on your device either way. We will not use them for advertising.

## Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control: you can view, change, export or erase your data in the App at any time (Settings → Erase all data), or remove it from your device by uninstalling the App.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when we introduce advertising, we will publish the new version at the same address, update the date below, and tell you in the App before the change takes effect.
